About me

Ever since my childhood, I’ve had a great interest in electronics, which has significantly shaped my path. Over time, that interest grew into a genuine passion for microelectronics—especially the fields of chip design, digital design, and semiconductor technology, which have sparked my curiosity the most.

These core areas were also the deciding factor in my decision to pursue a degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at the Graz University of Technology (TU Graz).

Driven by a high level of motivation and a structured way of working, I am always eager to develop my skills and take on new challenges in the hardware space.
